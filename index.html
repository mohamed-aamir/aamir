About Me

I am a sophomore PhD scholar in the Department of Ocean Engineering at IIT Madras, working under the guidance of Dr. K. Narendran. My research focuses on the intersection of hydrodynamics and structural stability of offshore systems.

Research Focus

Moored Floating Offshore Structures

I am currently investigating the dynamics and stability of moored systems in varied sea states. My work involves numerical modeling and computational analysis to improve the reliability of next-generation offshore platforms.
